# Palette Journal Entry - VersionModal Decorative Telemetry Brackets Accessibility

## Date
2026-11-01

## Micro-UX / Accessibility Improvement
Wrapped decorative telemetry brackets (`[` and `]`) in `VersionModal.tsx` around diagnostic readout tags (`ANOMALY`, `ACTION`) and version selection indicators (`SELECT`) with `<span aria-hidden="true">`.

## Key Learnings
- **Screen Reader Clarity on Diagnostic & Modal Controls**: Interactive modals and conflict resolution screens (such as `VersionModal`) that display telemetry readouts formatted with ASCII brackets (e.g., `[ ANOMALY ]`, `[ ACTION ]`, `[ SELECT ]`) should isolate bracket characters within `<span aria-hidden="true">`.
- This eliminates acoustic clutter by preventing screen readers from reciting "left bracket" and "right bracket" before critical messages and action prompts, preserving tactical hardware aesthetics visually while optimizing assistive technology output.

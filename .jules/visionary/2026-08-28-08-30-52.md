# Visionary Journal

## Learnings
- Focus on turning mathematically complex or tedious game mechanics into satisfying, actionable checklists (e.g., Feebas Tile Predictor, Berry Farming Tracker, PC Box Assistant).
- Downstream tracking nodes (Epics/Stories/Tasks) should not be created simultaneously with IDEAs; they are generated by subsequent personas in the pipeline (Epic Planner -> Story Owner -> Tech Lead).
- The `owner_persona` of new IDEA nodes must be set to `product_manager` to ensure the orchestrator picks it up for the next pipeline stage (PRD generation).

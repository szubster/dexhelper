- **Proposed Idea:** Gen 2 Headbutt Tree Predictor (IDEA-420)
- **Rationale & Concept:**
  In Gen 2 games, rare Headbutt trees (needed to catch Heracross or Pineco) are determined dynamically based on the player's Trainer ID. Finding them manually involves tedious trial-and-error. By extracting the Trainer ID from the save file and applying the in-game formula, DexHelper can predict the exact locations of these trees and highlight them on a map, providing an immediate solution to a painful gameplay loop.

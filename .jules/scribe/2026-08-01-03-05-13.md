# Scribe Journal - Gen 3 JSDoc\n\nWhen creating JSDoc for complex binary parsing logic, ensure you document the 'why' and the specific bitwise math. Bitwise operations like `(flag >> FLAG_BYTE_SHIFT)` and `(flag & FLAG_BIT_MASK)` are non-obvious to standard UI developers and warrant explicit inline comments. Also, when extracting boolean states from a dense event flags block (e.g. Move Tutors), document the multi-byte block structure used to fetch the data.

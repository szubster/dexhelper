## Critical Learnings
- **Dependency-Cruiser TypeScript Compatibility**: When `package.json` specifies a `typescript` version outside of dependency-cruiser's peer dependency range (e.g., `>=2.0.0 <7.0.0`), dependency-cruiser emits a warning (`missing-typescript-transpiler`) and silently skips analysis of all `.ts`/`.tsx` files (`0 modules, 0 dependencies cruised`).
- **Action Required**: Ensure root `package.json` specifies a supported TypeScript version range (`^5.x`). When updating TypeScript, ensure `tsconfig.json` includes `dom.iterable` under `compilerOptions.lib` to maintain `NodeListOf` iteration support.

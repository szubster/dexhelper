# Oak - Private Journal

## Learnings & Observations
- **Gen 3 Exclusivity Nuances**: The Gen 3 version exclusivity lists (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald and FireRed/LeafGreen) are notoriously tricky because the "missing" lists for the third version (Emerald) are not simply a combination of the first two (Ruby/Sapphire). For instance, Emerald has its own unique missing list (Surskit line, Meditite line, Roselia, Zangoose, Lunatone), while Pokémon like Seedot and Lotad are catchable in Emerald despite being exclusive to Ruby and Sapphire respectively.
- **FireRed/LeafGreen Cross-Gen Evolutions**: Cross-generational evolutions (like Magby/Elekid/Azurill introduced in Gen 2) follow the exclusivity of their evolutionary family lines (Magmar/Electabuzz/Marill) in the Gen 1 remakes, making them implicitly version exclusive when trading from other games or breeding.
- **Data Regeneration**: The data pipeline uses `pnpm data:gen` to pull directly from PokeAPI and regenerate the local JSONL files in `data/db/`. Always patch the source of truth (`scripts/generate-pokedata.ts` or `engine/exclusives/`) and let the generation script handle the downstream compilation to maintain the Single Source of Truth architecture and ensure the UI uses accurate `getUnobtainableReason` evaluations.

# Oak Data Integrity Learnings

- **Gen 3 Exclusives**: Pokémon Emerald's version exclusives are NOT simply the union of Ruby and Sapphire exclusions. While Pokémon like Seedot, Lotad, Mawile, Sableye, Seviper, and Solrock are available in Emerald (despite being exclusive to either Ruby or Sapphire), others like Surskit, Masquerain, Meditite, Medicham, Roselia, Zangoose, and Lunatone are completely unobtainable in Emerald and must be traded in. PokeAPI encounter data can be used to accurately determine availability per version.

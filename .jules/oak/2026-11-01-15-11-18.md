# Oak Journal - Data Integrity Audit

## Timestamp: 2026-11-01-15-11-18

### Domain Verified: Gen 1 Location/Encounter Mappings & PokeAPI Area ID Alignments

### Critical Learnings & Data Pipeline Gotchas:
- **PokeAPI Location Area Naming & ID Resolution:**
  - In PokeAPI, water routes in Kanto are named `kanto-sea-route-19` (area ID 277), `kanto-sea-route-20` (area ID 278), and `kanto-sea-route-21` (area ID 312).
  - In `scripts/data/gen1/mapping.ts`, Route 19 and Route 20 were erroneously assigned area IDs 314 (`kanto-route-24-area`) and 315 (`kanto-route-25-area`), duplicating the area IDs of Route 24 and Route 25. This caused sea routes 19 and 20 to inherit encounter data intended for terrestrial grass routes 24 and 25 (e.g., Weedle, Caterpie, Bellsprout).
  - Furthermore, Route 21 was assigned area ID 732 (`kalos-route-21-area`) from Generation 6 instead of Kanto's `kanto-sea-route-21-area` (area ID 312). Because Generation 6 encounters do not exist for Gen 1 game versions (Red/Blue/Yellow), Route 21 wound up with 0 encounters in the generated `encounters.jsonl` database.
  - Correcting the area IDs in `scripts/data/gen1/mapping.ts` to `277`, `278`, and `312` restored canonical Gen 1 water route encounters (e.g. Tentacool, Shellder, Magikarp, Tangela, Gyarados) for Routes 19, 20, and 21 across Red, Blue, and Yellow, while eliminating duplicated Route 24/25 grass encounters.
  - Always verify PokeAPI `location-area` endpoints against canonical URL patterns (e.g. `kanto-sea-route-*`) when constructing ROM ID to PokeAPI area mappings to avoid cross-generation ID collisions.

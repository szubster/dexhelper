## 2026-08-27 - [Accepted] - Redesign Pokedex Cards as Data Cartridges
**What:** Transformed PokedexCard and TacticalCard layouts into a top-to-bottom 'Data Cartridge' structural aesthetic, utilizing physical corners with structural hardware screws, a 3D inset drop-shadow rim (simulating thickness), and depression effects on interaction states.
**Outcome:** Accepted
**Why:** Improves tactile and diegetic connection for heavy snooping UI components by simulating physical cartridges slotted into a hardware matrix instead of flat UI side-by-side elements.
**Pattern:** Combine thick raised borders with 3D drop-shadows `shadow-[0_4px_0_rgba(...)]`, `translate-y` interaction depressions on click, and simulated metallic screws in the corners `shadow-[inset_0_1px_0_rgba(255,255,255,0.2)]` for physical UI elements.

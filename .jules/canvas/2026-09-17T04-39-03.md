## 2026-09-17 - [Accepted] - 🖼️ Canvas: Bold DiagnosticCard Redesign
**What:** Transformed the `DiagnosticCard` component from a semi-open tactical node into a fully enclosed, highly structured tactical panel. The new design includes dashed borders, corner crosshairs, LCD grid backgrounds, a scanner overlay on hover, and a primary label positioned inside technical brackets at the top-left.
**Outcome:** Pending review.
**Why:** The previous design lacked strong boundaries and felt slightly disconnected from the heavy hardware aesthetic. The redesign emphasizes encapsulation and interactivity (hover states) to make the diagnostic metrics feel like they are displayed on physical hardware screens.
**Pattern:** Continued the pattern established by the `DataPoint` redesign, favoring enclosed, bordered components with crosshairs and background overlays to reinforce the tactical hardware aesthetic.

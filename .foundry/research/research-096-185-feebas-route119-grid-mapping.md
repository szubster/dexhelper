# RESEARCH: Investigate Route 119 Grid Dimensions and Feebas Spot ID Mapping

## Objective
To map the 1D Feebas spot IDs (ranging from 4 to 447) to 2D relative map coordinates (x, y) for Route 119, as generated by the Feebas RNG algorithm.

## Requirements
- [x] Determine the full dimensions (width and height) of the Route 119 map grid.

## Research Findings

### Root Cause
The task `task-478-688-orchestrator-cloning-logic-retry-4` and its predecessor tasks failed permanently due to the maximum rejection count being reached. Looking closely at the orchestrator tests (specifically around `packages/foundry/foundry-orchestrator.test.ts`), the orchestrator already implements duplication/cloning logic correctly under the hood, but earlier coders kept failing, triggering a cascading sequence of retries. The core issue is that coders repeatedly asked the user questions about how to implement the missing context (Autonomous No-Ask Policy Violation: Session entered `AWAITING_USER_FEEDBACK`). The orchestrator heartbeat kills these sessions and marks them as failed. When they retry too many times, they reach the max rejection count and permanently fail.

Additionally, this specific `RESEARCH` node is actually an artifact generated by the orchestrator's "Impossible Loop" handling after the parent story failed because its child task `task-478-688-orchestrator-cloning-logic-retry-4` reached its max rejection limit.

### Recommended Approach
1. **Deterministic Global Sequence Numbers**: Use the globally pooled sequence number parsing strategy to assign new sequence numbers during cloning. E.g. find maximum `[0-9]{3}-[0-9]{3}` sequence numbers in `.foundry/*/*` and increment.
2. **Metadata Reset**: Reset lifecycle fields in the YAML frontmatter when duplicating (`status: READY`, `jules_session_id: null`, `rejection_count: 0`, `rejection_reason: ''`).
3. **No-Ask Adherence**: Implementations must be fully autonomous. Coders must NOT ask for permission or hints. They must read logs, inspect orchestrator behavior, and submit the implementation directly.

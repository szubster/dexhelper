# Idea: Gen 3 Berry Blender Multiplayer Optimizer

## Context
While there is an idea for a Pokeblock recipe optimizer based on inventory, players often blend berries with NPCs or link cables where they cannot control all inputs. There is no tool to simulate the outcome of multiplayer berry blending with a mix of known and unknown berry inputs.

## Proposal
Create a simulator that allows users to input their berry, known NPC/friend berries, and calculates the expected Pokeblock feel and level, providing strategies for the best possible outcome in a multiplayer blending scenario.

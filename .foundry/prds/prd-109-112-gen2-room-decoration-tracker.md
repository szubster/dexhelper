# PRD: Gen 2 Room Decoration & Mom's Savings Tracker

## Overview
Introduce a "Room Decoration & Savings" dashboard for Gen 2 save files (Gold, Silver, Crystal) in the DexHelper app. This will allow players to easily track their unlocked room decorations and Mom's savings progress without manually checking their PC in-game.

## Requirements
1. **Save Block Extraction:** Parse the exact event flags and memory blocks associated with room decorations and Mom's bank account for Gen 2 saves.
2. **Decoration Inventory Viewer:** Provide a categorized view (Beds, Plants, Posters, Consoles, Ornaments, Dolls) of all unlocked room decorations against the complete list of possible decorations.
3. **Mom's Savings Tracker:** Show the current monetary value saved by Mom.
4. **Unlock Progress UI:** Display the progression towards the next decoration threshold unlocked via Mom's savings.
5. **Mystery Gift Tracker:** Visually highlight decorations that are exclusively obtainable via Mystery Gift.

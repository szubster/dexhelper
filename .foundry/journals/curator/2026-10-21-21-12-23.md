## Learnings
Even if an Idea or PRD fails due to a session timeout or Orchestrator crash during its Empty PR submission step, its functionality may have already been completely merged by its children in previous attempts or parallel sessions. Before triggering a retry loop or attempting to re-implement, always verify the state of the child nodes and the codebase. Since the feature is fully integrated, the node just needs its checkboxes updated and submitted via an Empty PR to transition to COMPLETED gracefully.

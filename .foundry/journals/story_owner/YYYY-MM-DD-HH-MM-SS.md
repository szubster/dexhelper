# Session Log: Story Owner

## Observations & Lessons Learned

- **Orchestrator Safeguard E2E Stories**: As dictated by the Orchestrator Safeguard policy, when planning or updating EPICs, I must explicitly ensure that at least one STORY is dedicated exclusively to Integration and E2E Verification. This ensures that the orchestrator does not prematurely fail the EPIC due to a lack of required coverage. I applied this rule by creating a new E2E story node for the PC Box Sorting Algorithms epic after discovering it lacked one.
